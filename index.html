faq

you probably have questions

what is slumbr?

SLUMBR is a sleep improvement app made around the way teens actually live. It combines sleep tracking with XP, streaks, ranks, challenges and friendly competition.

is slumbr just a sleep tracker?

No. Tracking is the starting point. SLUMBR is designed to help you actually build consistency and make progress feel rewarding.

do i need a smartwatch?

No. You can manually log your sleep. Health and wearable integrations can come later.

is slumbr free?

The core experience is planned to be free. Basic sleep tracking shouldn’t be locked behind a paywall.

can i compete with my friends?

Yep. Private friend groups, seasonal rankings and challenges are part of the vision.

does slumbr punish me for a bad night?

No. One rough night shouldn’t ruin everything. SLUMBR is about getting back on track, not shaming you.
